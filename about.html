About Dr. Swati Garekar

Meet the Doctor

Dr. Swati Garekar is a Consultant Pediatric Cardiologist based in Mumbai, Maharashtra. She currently practices at the Fortis Child Heart Mission (FCHM), Fortis Hospital in Mulund, Mumbai, where she has been since September 2012.

Her extensive training includes an American Board Certified 3-year fellowship in Pediatric Cardiology (2002-2005) and a 3-year residency in Pediatrics, both completed at the Children's Hospital of Michigan, United States. She earned her MBBS from KEM Hospital, Seth Gordhandas Sunderdas Medical College.

Prior to her current position, Dr. Garekar served as a Consultant Pediatric Cardiologist at the Children's Heart Centre, Kokilaben Dhirubhai Ambani Hospital, Mumbai (2009-2012) and Wockhardt Hospital, Mumbai (2006-2009).

Dr. Garekar possesses strong skills in healthcare, research, and public speaking, and is fluent in Marathi and Hindi.
